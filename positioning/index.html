What Data 360 is, and is not

Does

  • Harmonizes disparate data
  • Unifies profiles with related data
  • Builds insights on unified profiles
  • Foundation for Agentforce
  • Segments harmonized data
  • Activates data to apps, workflows, channels and agents

Is not

  • An ETL or middleware tool
  • A data cleansing tool
  • MDM (master data management)
  • A way to build a golden record
  • A data governance tool
  • Backup and disaster recovery
  • A business intelligence platform

AI vocabulary in four lines

Predictive AI

  • Uses historical data to estimate likely outcomes

Generative AI

  • Produces summaries, answers and responses

Trust and consent

  • Einstein Trust Layer

    Secure data retrieval, dynamic grounding, data masking, zero data retention, toxic language detection, audit trail. It protects generative AI. It does not replace governance, consent or business judgment, and it does not make weak data trustworthy.

  • Consent layers

    Individual or party (who), channel (email, SMS, phone, mail), contact point (which address or number), purpose (service, billing, marketing, survey). One customer can allow service email to one address, block marketing SMS to a mobile, and still allow billing notices.

  • Data Use Purpose

    The reason for contacting or using data. Access to data does not mean every use is allowed.

  • Consent API

    Finds consent across related records and writes consent values, including Data Use Purpose and the Data Cloud Individual record. It helps honor the most restrictive preference.

  • Data minimization

    Collect and keep only what the purpose needs. A benefit reminder needs account type, unused-benefit flag, recent engagement and consent status. Full transaction history is too much. An email address alone is not enough.

Strong versus weak Phase 1

Strong

  • Clear business problem with a named owner
  • One primary KPI
  • Data is available or reachable
  • Clear downstream action or activation path
  • Narrow enough for a first release
  • Explainable in a sentence
  • Manageable consent and governance

Weak

  • Starts with a feature
  • Too many goals, teams and metrics
  • Hard-to-reach or poor-quality data
  • No action after the insight
  • Whole enterprise in one step
  • Governance bolted on later

Five-part Phase 1 scorecard

  • Business value

    Is there a real KPI and an owner?

  • Data readiness

    Usable data and keys?

  • Activation path

    A clear downstream action?

  • Delivery scope

    Small enough to land?

  • Governance fit

    Clear purpose, consent and minimization?

Watch for these

  • “Not MDM” and “not a golden record” are the same idea: Data 360 links records, it does not replace the system of record.
  • A hypothesis drives a good use case: what are we improving, what experience, who, when in the lifecycle, how measured?
  • Test-and-learn: hypothesis, experiment, observation, conclusion, repeat.
